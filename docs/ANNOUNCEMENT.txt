================================================================================
GeoDjengs - Nama Baru, Pengalaman Lebih Baik
================================================================================

RINGKASAN
---------
Aplikasi Geotag Kamera kini resmi berganti nama menjadi GeoDjengs. Rebranding
ini menandai peningkatan tampilan, peta, dan penyempurnaan watermark foto.
Semua data dan alur kerja Anda tetap sama.

YANG BARU
---------
- Identitas baru: nama, logo, dan favicon GeoDjengs menggantikan branding lama.
- Tampilan lebih segar: refresh desain pada header, tab bar, tombol kamera,
  kartu alamat, dan halaman peta.
- Peta OpenStreetMap: mini-map watermark kini memakai OpenStreetMap, ringan
  dan tanpa kunci API.
- Watermark lebih ringkas: panel info pada foto menyusut dari 1/4 menjadi 1/5
  tinggi foto, sehingga subjek foto lebih dominan.
- Alur izin lebih jelas: halaman permission kamera dan lokasi ditata ulang
  agar langkahnya lebih mudah diikuti.

YANG TETAP SAMA
---------------
- Semua fungsi inti: capture foto, geotagging otomatis, watermark alamat,
  koordinat, waktu, dan mini-map.
- Dukungan dwi-bahasa (Indonesia dan English).
- Instalasi PWA dari browser, dengan dukungan offline untuk foto yang sudah
  di-capture.

TINDAKAN YANG DISARANKAN
------------------------
1. Jika sudah memasang versi sebelumnya sebagai PWA, tutup lalu buka ulang
   tab untuk memuat aset terbaru.
2. Pasang ulang ikon home screen (opsional) agar logo baru GeoDjengs muncul
   di perangkat.
3. Cek hasil capture: panel watermark kini lebih ramping; beri masukan ke
   tim jika Anda ingin kembali ke ukuran lama.


================================================================================
GeoDjengs - New Name, Better Experience
================================================================================

SUMMARY
-------
The Geotag Camera app is now officially GeoDjengs. This rebrand ships a
visual refresh, map improvements, and a slimmer photo watermark. Nothing
about your data or workflow changes.

WHAT'S NEW
----------
- New identity: GeoDjengs name, logo, and favicon replace the old branding.
- Fresh UI: redesigned header, tab bar, capture button, address card, and
  map page.
- OpenStreetMap mini-map: watermark map switches to OSM, lighter and
  key-free.
- Slimmer watermark: info panel reduced from 1/4 to 1/5 of the photo height,
  keeping your subject more prominent.
- Clearer permissions flow: camera and location permission screens rewritten
  for faster onboarding.

WHAT STAYS THE SAME
-------------------
- All core features: capture, auto geotagging, watermark with address,
  coordinates, timestamp, and mini-map.
- Bilingual support (Indonesian and English).
- PWA install from the browser with offline support for already-captured
  photos.

SUGGESTED ACTIONS
-----------------
1. If you installed the old version as a PWA, close and reopen the tab to
   pick up the new assets.
2. Reinstall the home-screen shortcut (optional) to see the new GeoDjengs
   icon.
3. Review recent captures: the watermark panel is slimmer; let the team
   know if you prefer the previous size.
